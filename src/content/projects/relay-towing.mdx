## About Project

**Relay Towing** is a modern dispatch and operations management platform designed specifically for towing service providers.  
It centralizes service calls, real-time driver dispatching, vehicle tracking, invoicing, and more into a unified, web-based dashboard.  
With features tailored to small- and mid-sized businesses, Relay Towing helps teams streamline workflows, boost efficiency, and stay on top of both customer communication and back-office operations.

## Client's Need

The client approached us with a clear mission: **transform outdated towing operations into a centralized digital experience** that would support both dispatchers and drivers.  

Their core requirements included:

- Designing a **clean and intuitive interface** for dispatchers and managers  
- Enabling **live tracking of driver locations** and service status  
- Building a **role-based system** for admins, dispatchers, and drivers  
- Developing a **robust backend** for handling jobs, users, billing, and alerts  
- Ensuring **reliable, real-time communication** across devices  
- Deploying with **cloud-native, scalable infrastructure**

## Solution

We engineered a platform with a **React-based frontend** and a **NestJS-powered backend**, ensuring seamless performance and modern UX.

Key components included:

- **Frontend on Vercel**: for rapid CI/CD, responsive UI, and global delivery  
- **Backend using NestJS**: modular architecture with REST APIs and real-time capabilities  
- **Database via Supabase/Postgres**: scalable and schema-flexible for user, dispatch, and invoice management  
- **Authentication with Auth0**: secure access control and role-based permissions  
- **Real-time GPS tracking**: using WebSockets and polling for dispatch visibility  
- **Mobile driver app (React Native - bare workflow)**: to optimize native push notifications and background services  
- **TestFlight QA process**: ensuring production-readiness before app store deployment  
- **Hosting on Railway**: enabling fast, serverless deployment with integrated database provisioning

## Impact

The Relay Towing platform achieved major operational improvements:

- **Reduced dispatch response time** through live tracking and rapid communication  
- **Boosted accountability** with real-time check-ins and status updates  
- **Improved user experience** with a streamlined UI and fewer manual processes  
- **Set the foundation for future growth**, including guardian alerts, analytics dashboards, and multi-location support  
- **High reliability** under load due to a cloud-native backend architecture

By combining clarity, speed, and real-time control, Relay Towing is reshaping how towing companies operate in the digital age.
