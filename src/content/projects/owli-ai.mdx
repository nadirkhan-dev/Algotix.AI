
### About Project


In the fast-moving world of digital content, creators often find themselves bogged down by the repetitive and time-consuming tasks involved in editing, enhancing, and distributing video content across multiple platforms. While tools like Repurpose.io help with automation, they still leave a gap in intelligent enhancement and personalization at scale — especially for solo creators and influencers who need to stand out.

Owli.ai was created to address this exact challenge. Co-founded with the vision of simplifying video content workflows, Owli.ai offers an intelligent, creator-first platform that not only repurposes video content but also enhances it automatically using advanced AI-driven features. With functionality like word-by-word subtitles, intelligent clip extraction, filters, and platform-specific optimizations, Owli.ai helps creators go from raw recording to multi-platform publishing — all within a few clicks.

---

## Client's Need

The client partnered with us to **build and enhance their platform**, focusing on delivering an **intuitive, user-friendly experience** that helps creators enhance and distribute their content efficiently.

Their core requirements included:

- **AI-driven video enhancements**  
- **Seamless multi-platform publishing**  
- **Improving overall platform performance and speed**  
- **Building a scalable, event-driven architecture**  
- **Optimizing backend to handle asynchronous video processing**

---

## Solution

To meet Owli.ai’s goals, we delivered a **seamless, easy-to-navigate interface** where creators could upload, enhance, and publish content in a few simple steps.

On the backend, we utilized:

- **AWS Amplify** and **Supabase/Postgres** for scalability and real-time data processing  
- Integration with **Vizard.ai** for automatic subtitle generation, smart clip extraction, and video enhancement  
- Real-time social media integrations for direct publishing and scheduling across platforms like Instagram, YouTube, and TikTok

We also focused on performance and security by implementing:

- **Event-driven architecture** using **AWS EventBridge**  
- **Containerized processing** with **AWS ECS** for efficient background job management  
- **Role-based access control** for secure video management and processing

The platform also included **real-time notifications** and **status updates**, ensuring users were always informed of their content’s progress.
