## About Project

**CSwap Systems** is a next-generation decentralized exchange (DEX) designed to **bridge the gap between tokens, NFTs, and real-world assets (RWAs)** within a unified trading platform.  
By offering a **hybrid model**, CSwap aims to provide **seamless trading experiences** and **greater accessibility** for users across the decentralized finance (DeFi) ecosystem.

## Client's Need

The client partnered with us to **build and refine their decentralized trading platform**, focusing on creating an **intuitive, user-friendly DEX** that **lowers entry barriers** for both beginners and experienced DeFi users.

Their core requirements included:

- Developing **secure token swap** functionality  
- Integrating **liquidity pool** features  
- Improving overall **platform performance**  
- Deploying a **scalable, blockchain-integrated** solution on **AWS infrastructure**

## Solution

To meet CSwap’s goals, we delivered a **sleek, responsive frontend** using **Next.js**, ensuring fast performance and SEO benefits.

On the backend, we utilized:

- **AWS Amplify** and **DynamoDB** for scalability and reliability  
- Integration with **Cardano blockchain** for native and non-native token support  
- Real-time trading with **live pricing feeds**

We also prioritized security by implementing:

- **Smart contract audits**  
- **Fail-safe mechanisms**  
- Protection against **front-running** and **flash loan exploits**

The platform included a **dual environment setup** (testnet and mainnet) and **CI/CD pipelines** for smooth deployment and continuous updates.
