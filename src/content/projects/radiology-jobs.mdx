## About Project 
**Radiology Jobs** is a niche job board platform created specifically for the radiology sector. It connects hospitals, clinics, imaging centers, and individual professionals in a centralized space designed to simplify and streamline the hiring process. The platform offers robust tools for **job postings, applicant tracking, and candidate management** through a clean, user-friendly interface — making recruitment in the radiology field faster and more efficient.

## Client's Need 
The client required a comprehensive platform tailored to the hiring workflows of the radiology industry. The goals included delivering a modern UI, integrating secure authentication and access control, supporting multiple user roles (recruiter, applicant, admin), and ensuring fast, scalable performance. Additionally, they wanted a separate admin interface to manage platform operations effectively.

## Solution 
To meet the client’s objectives, we built a full-stack application using **Next.js**, utilizing both frontend and backend capabilities of the framework to provide a smooth user experience. The backend was architected with **RAW SQL queries** on a **MySQL database**, giving us full control over data operations and optimized performance. For deployment, we used **DigitalOcean**, configuring a secure and production-ready environment with attention to scalability, uptime, and security best practices.

## Impact
The platform has had a significant positive impact on both recruiters and job seekers in the radiology industry.  
- **Streamlined Recruitment:** Recruiters can now manage listings and candidates from one efficient interface.  
- **Candidate Visibility:** Radiology professionals have a focused space to highlight their expertise and get discovered by top employers.  
- **Industry Optimization:** Tailored exclusively for radiology, the platform avoids irrelevant listings and improves satisfaction on both sides.  
- **Scalable Foundation:** With a reliable infrastructure in place, the system is primed for future expansion — including features like credential verification, analytics, and advanced filtering.
