
## About Project
OceanTech is redefining IT asset decommissioning (ITAD) with secure, sustainable, and client-centric solutions. Since 2005, they’ve served businesses, government agencies, and Fortune 500 companies across North America — focusing on value recovery and minimizing e-waste through responsible recycling and reuse. A new website project aimed to showcase their leadership in ITAD, simplify lead generation, and enable dynamic content management.

## Client's Need
The client sought a complete digital overhaul to modernize their web presence. Their legacy website was outdated, slow, and difficult to manage. They needed a sleek, scalable platform that supported dynamic content, streamlined customer interaction, and laid the groundwork for future expansion — including client portals and multilingual support.

## Solution

- **Frontend Development:**  
  Built a fast, SEO-optimized website with **Next.js**, using static generation and server-side rendering (SSR) for better performance and discoverability.

- **Content Management Integration:**  
  Integrated **Contentful CMS** to give the marketing team complete editorial control over blogs, service pages, and updates — without developer input.

- **Chatbot and Forms Integration:**  
  Implemented **HubSpot** for real-time chatbot support, automated lead capture, and intuitive form submission handling.

- **Performance and Scalability:**  
  Deployed the platform on **Vercel**, ensuring fast load times and a global edge network. CI/CD pipelines were set up for smooth releases and future flexibility.

## Impact

- **Enhanced Brand Image:**  
  The modern platform helped OceanTech stand out as a forward-thinking, eco-conscious ITAD leader, instilling greater trust with enterprise clients.

- **Improved Lead Capture:**  
  HubSpot chatbot and forms made it easier for users to engage, submit inquiries, and convert into leads  boosting inbound activity.

- **Empowered Content Team:**  
  Marketing gained the ability to publish new content instantly, accelerating campaigns and improving agility without relying on developers.

- **Future-Ready Platform:**  
  With a flexible stack and scalable infrastructure, the site is ready for client portals, asset tracking, and multilingual expansion.
